Bloomberg reported on March 11, 2025 that Mark Fedorcik, former head of Deutsche Bank's investment division, has joined Standard Industries as Chief Financial Officer. He succeeds John Maitner, who is retiring after nearly 25 years in leadership roles at Standard Industries and its operating companies.
