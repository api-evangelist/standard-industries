Standard Industries is a privately-held global industrial company headquartered in New York City and tracing its origins to 1886. The company operates a portfolio of building solutions, performance materials, logistics, real estate, and investment businesses, with more than 20,000 employees and approximately $11 billion in annual revenues. Standard Building Solutions includes GAF (North America's largest roofing manufacturer), GAF Energy (Timberline Solar nailable solar shingles), BMI Group (Europe's largest pitched and flat roofing manufacturer, with brands including Braas, Monier, Icopal, Wolfin, and Redland), Siplast (specialty roofing and waterproofing), SGI (mining and mineral processing), Schiedel (flue, stove, and ventilation systems), and Standard Logistics. Performance Materials is anchored by W. R. Grace & Co., which produces catalysts, engineered materials, process technologies, and fine chemicals. Standard Investments deploys capital across public equities and early-to-growth venture investments. Winter Properties is a vertically integrated real estate firm with more than 100 years of history in New York City. Standard Industries does not publish a public developer API or corporate developer portal; technology integration with customers and partners is handled inside the individual operating companies.
