GAF Energy completed construction of its Timberline Solar Energy Shingle manufacturing plant in Georgetown, Texas, with full operations expected by year-end 2023. The facility increases production capacity by 500% to 300 megawatts annually, positioning GAF Energy as the world's largest solar roofing manufacturer. Over 75 employees were hired, with plans to grow to more than 240 at full capacity.
