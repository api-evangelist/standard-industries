Standard Industries and its specialty chemicals company W. R. Grace & Co. were named to Newsweek's 2026 "America's Greatest Workplaces" and "America's Greatest Workplaces in Manufacturing" lists. Grace earned additional 2026 distinctions including "America's Most Admired Workplaces," "America's Greatest Workplaces for Women," and "America's Greatest Workplaces for Culture, Belonging & Community."
