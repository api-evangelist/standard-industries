Standard Industries and W. R. Grace & Co. launched a competition on March 7, 2024 offering $1 million to the individual or team that best uses AI to transform how fine chemicals are manufactured. The initiative focuses on enhancing chemical retrosynthesis through artificial intelligence and machine learning.
